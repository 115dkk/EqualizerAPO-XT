These parts of EqualizerAPO-XT were adapted from Custom Audio Manager. The
implementation in EqualizerAPO-XT is independent and does not include JUCE
code.

- the VST non-finite output guard and post-activation latency refresh
- the audio-path allocation test
- skipping the reopen of a plug-in panel that was open when the Editor crashed
- the missing and repeated capture frame counters of the WASAPI-exclusive
  target
- the Send receive resampler and its drift controller
